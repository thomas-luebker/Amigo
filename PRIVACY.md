# iPadUAE — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 20 July 2026**

iPadUAE is a classic Amiga emulator for iPad, developed by Thomas Lübker.

## The short version

iPadUAE collects **no data**. None. There are no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising, no tracking, and no telemetry of any kind.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app does not collect, store, or transmit
  any personal information, usage statistics, crash reports, or
  identifiers to the developer or to any third party.
- **No network communication by the app.** The app itself makes no
  internet connections — no update checks, no content downloads, no
  analytics endpoints.
- **Emulated networking.** If you enable the optional network feature,
  software running *inside the emulated Amiga* can access the internet
  through your device's connection, exactly as a web browser would when
  you use it. The app does not inspect, log, or transmit this traffic
  anywhere else.
- **Your files stay yours.** Disk images, Kickstart ROMs, configurations
  and save states are stored in the app's folder on your device, visible
  in the Files app and under your control. They are never uploaded by
  the app.
- **Bluetooth.** The app uses Bluetooth solely to connect game
  controllers for input. No Bluetooth data is collected or transmitted.

## App Store privacy label

Consistent with the above, the App Store listing declares
**"Data Not Collected."**

## Changes

Any future change to this policy will be published at this address and
